The Pink City Weekend

📍 Jaipur, Rajasthan · Western India

A short, vibrant cultural escape through Jaipur’s grand architecture, bustling bazaars, and historic palace courtyards. Experience the grandeur of Amber Fort and the intricate facade of Hawa Mahal.

Duration 3 Days / 2 Nights
Rating ★ 4.7
Theme Culture
Transport Private AC Sedan

Your Journey, Day by Day

1
Day 1: Arrive & Explore Historic Bazaars Arrival

Check-in at your heritage hotel. In the afternoon, head into the Pink City walled area to explore Johari Bazaar for silver jewelry and Bapu Bazaar for handcrafted textiles.

2
Day 2: Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal & City Palace Grand Palaces

Ascend to the hilltop Amer Fort, visit the Sheesh Mahal (Mirror Palace), view Jal Mahal floating in Man Sagar Lake, and tour City Palace museum.

3
Day 3: Rajasthani Breakfast & Departure Departure

Enjoy a traditional breakfast of pyaaz kachoris and lassi, pick up last-minute bandhani souvenirs, and transfer to Jaipur airport or railway junction.
