Kerala, at Your Own Pace

📍 Alleppey & Munnar, Kerala · South India

Seven restful days from the misty tea plantations and waterfalls of Munnar down to the calm, palm-shaded backwaters of Alleppey. Designed for those seeking tranquility, clean air, and restorative nature.

Duration 7 Days / 6 Nights
Rating ★ 4.9
Theme Nature
Transport Private AC Cab
₹22,500 per person · Twin Sharing (incl. taxes)

Your Journey, Day by Day

1
Day 1: Arrive in Cochin & Scenic Drive to Munnar Arrival

Meet your driver at Cochin airport and drive through rubber plantations and Cheeyappara waterfalls up to Munnar hill station.

2
Day 2: Tea Estates & Eravikulam Wildlife Nature

Visit the Tata Tea Museum for tea tasting, explore rolling green hills, and spot the endangered Nilgiri Tahr at Eravikulam National Park.

3
Day 3: Mattupetty Dam & Spice Gardens Exploration

Take a calm boat ride across Mattupetty Dam, try your voice at Echo Point, and stroll through aromatic cardamom and clove spice plantations.

4
Day 4: Descent to Alleppey & Lakeside Sunset Transit

Descend from the Western Ghats to the coastal plains of Alleppey. Check into your waterside heritage resort and enjoy an evening Ayurvedic foot massage.

5
Day 5: Overnight Deluxe Houseboat Cruise Houseboat

Board your private houseboat. Glide through Vembanad Lake while the on-board chef prepares fresh Karimeen fish and traditional Kerala vegetarian delicacies.

6
Day 6: Village Canoeing & Coir Weaving Trail Culture

Wake to sunrise over the backwaters. Take a small country canoe through narrow village canals and observe local duck farming and coir making.

7
Day 7: Fort Kochi Walk & Departure Departure

Stop by Fort Kochi to see the historic Chinese fishing nets and Jewish Synagogue before your final transfer to Cochin International Airport.

The Little Details
