Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our holiday packages, transparent INR pricing, booking process, and travel support.

How do I book a tour package with Roam India?

Booking with Roam India follows a straightforward 3-step process:

  1. Navigate to the Packages page or pick any featured destination circuit.
  2. Click "Book Now" to configure your travel dates, party size, and room preferences.
  3. Complete checkout to receive an instant digital booking confirmation and travel reference voucher.
Are the displayed tour prices all-inclusive?

Yes. All rates displayed across Roam India are shown in Indian Rupees (INR) and include verified hotel accommodation (twin-sharing), daily breakfast, private chauffeur-driven transfers for scheduled sightseeing, monument entries as listed, and applicable GST and state tourism taxes. No hidden convenience fees are added at checkout.

Can I customize an existing itinerary?

Yes! Our packages are designed to be flexible. Travelers can easily adjust the number of days, upgrade accommodation categories (from comfortable 3-star stays to heritage havelis or premium houseboats), or add custom excursions. Simply submit a request via our Contact Form.

What payment methods are supported?

We support multiple convenient digital payment methods including instant UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), major Credit and Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay), and Net Banking across all leading Indian banks.

How does the package comparison tool work?

Our dedicated Compare Page lines up our top travel circuits side-by-side in an easy-to-read table. You can evaluate per-person tariffs, trip durations, included meals, private transport types, activity highlights, and optimal visiting seasons at a single glance.

What is typically included in a Roam India package?

Standard packages include handpicked hotel accommodations, daily breakfast, listed monument entry passes, dedicated private air-conditioned vehicle transfers throughout the itinerary, and 24/7 on-trip helpline support. Long-distance flights or trains from your hometown are self-arranged and excluded by default.

What is the cancellation and refund policy?

Cancellations requested at least 15 days prior to the journey departure date qualify for a 90% refund. Cancellations made 7 to 14 days before departure receive a 50% refund. Cancellations within 7 days of departure are non-refundable due to hotel commitments.

Can I modify my travel dates after booking?

Date modifications are accepted up to 10 days before your scheduled departure date without penalty, subject to seasonal hotel rate adjustments and room availability. Reach out to our customer support desk with your booking reference ID to process date changes.

How can I contact customer support?

You can reach our dedicated travel assistance team via the Contact Page, by emailing support@roamindia.example, or by calling our toll-free helpline at +91 1800 123 7626 (Monday through Saturday, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM IST).
