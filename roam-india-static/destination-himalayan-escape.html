The Himalayan Escape

📍 Manali & Solang Valley, Himachal Pradesh

Trade the everyday for pine-scented trails, snow-lined peaks, and slow mornings in the high mountains. Experience cedar forest walks, ancient temples, cable car thrills, and scenic high-altitude viewpoints.

Your Journey, Day by Day

1
Day 1: Arrive in Manali & Old Manali Walk Arrival

Check-in at your mountain resort, unwind with hot apple cider, and explore the quaint wooden architecture and riverside pathways of Old Manali.

2
Day 2: Cedar Forests & Hadimba Temple Heritage

Walk beneath the giant century-old deodar trees to Hadimba Temple, visit the Tibetan Monastery, and taste traditional Kullu siddu.

3
Day 3: Solang Valley Mountain Adventures Adventure

Spend the day at Solang Valley enjoying the high-altitude ropeway, zorbing, snow activities, and panoramic glacier photography.

4
Day 4: Naggar Castle & Heritage Village Culture

Take a picturesque drive to medieval Naggar Castle, visit the Nicholas Roerich Art Gallery, and enjoy lunch overlooking apple orchards.

5
Day 5: Vashisht Hot Springs & Local Markets Leisure

Relax in the natural therapeutic hot springs at Vashisht village, followed by evening souvenir shopping along Mall Road.

6
Day 6: Breakfast & Departure Departure

Enjoy a slow breakfast overlooking the snowy peaks before your private transfer to the departure terminal.
