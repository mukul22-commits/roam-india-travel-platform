Backwater Hideaway

📍 Alleppey, Kerala · South India

Ease into waterside mornings, gentle canal ripples, and quiet village walks around Kerala’s iconic backwater network. Unwind on a deluxe traditional houseboat with authentic freshly prepared coastal cuisine.

Duration 4 Days / 3 Nights
Rating ★ 4.8
Theme Relaxation
Stay Type Houseboat & Resort
₹12,900 per person · Twin Sharing (incl. taxes)

Your Journey, Day by Day

1
Day 1: Arrival in Alleppey & Canal Welcome Arrival

Arrive in Alleppey from Cochin. Check in to your serene lakeside resort, sip on fresh tender coconut water, and take in the golden sunset over the backwater lake.

2
Day 2: Boarding the Traditional Houseboat Houseboat Cruise

Board your private thatched-roof Kettuvallam houseboat at 12 noon. Cruise through the narrow palm-shaded canals of Vembanad Lake while savoring authentic Kerala fish curry or vegetarian sadhya.

3
Day 3: Village Walks & Coir Craft Trail Local Culture

Wake to sunrise over the water. Step off for a guided morning walk through riverside hamlets, watch traditional coir spinning, and enjoy an afternoon country canoe excursion.

4
Day 4: Breakfast & Departure Departure

Relish a warm breakfast of appams and stew before disembarking for your private transfer back to Cochin airport or railway terminal.

The Little Details

Package Inclusions

  • 3 nights accommodation (1 night deluxe houseboat + 2 nights waterside resort)
  • Daily breakfast plus all meals on board the houseboat
  • Sunset village canoe excursion
  • Private AC cab for all transfers from Cochin

Not Included

  • Personal expenses, Ayurvedic spa massages, laundry
