A Little Mountain Reset

📍 Manali, Himachal Pradesh · North India

A short, invigorating 3-day weekend breather designed to help travelers disconnect. Breathe in pine-scented air, sip warm mountain tea in Old Manali, and listen to the rushing Beas River.

Duration 3 Days / 2 Nights
Rating ★ 4.6
Theme Hills
Transport Local AC Cab
₹8,900 per person · Twin Sharing (incl. taxes)
Book This Trip ↗ Compare Tours

Your Journey, Day by Day

1
Day 1: Arrival & Old Manali Cafe Stroll Arrival

Reach Manali by morning bus or private cab. Check into your cozy hillside resort, unwind by the cedar trees, and head out to Old Manali for live acoustic music and wood-fired pizza.

2
Day 2: Hadimba Woods & Vashisht Hot Springs Nature & Springs

Walk the shaded trail through Hadimba Temple forest sanctuary, take a therapeutic bath in Vashisht sulphur springs, and enjoy an evening riverside bonfire at the resort.

3
Day 3: Mountain Breakfast & Departure Departure

Enjoy hot parathas and ginger lemon honey tea overlooking the mist-covered valley before heading to the Volvo bus terminal for your return trip.

The Little Details

Package Inclusions

  • 2 nights stay in a valley-facing boutique hotel
  • Daily buffet breakfast
  • Hadimba Temple entry and local taxi allowance
  • Evening riverside bonfire setup

Not Included

  • Volvo bus or flight fare to Manali
  • Lunches, dinners, and cafe bills
  • Optional adventure sports permits
