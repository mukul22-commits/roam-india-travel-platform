A Royal Rajasthan Trail

📍 Jaipur & Udaipur, Rajasthan · Western India

Discover Rajasthan's regal majesty across five curated days. From the sandstone battlements of Amer Fort and Hawa Mahal in the Pink City to the shimmering waters and white marble havelis of Lake Pichola in Udaipur.

Duration 5 Days / 4 Nights
Rating ★ 4.8
Theme Culture
Stay Type Heritage Haveli
₹16,400 per person · Twin Sharing (incl. taxes)

Your Journey, Day by Day

1
Day 1: Arrive in Jaipur & Heritage Check-in Arrival

Arrive in the Pink City. Settle into your boutique haveli hotel, enjoy a welcome glass of spiced buttermilk, and take an evening stroll around Jal Mahal lake.

2
Day 2: Amer Fort, Hawa Mahal & Bazaars Forts & Craft

Ascend to majestic Amer Fort with a licensed historian guide. Photograph Hawa Mahal's honeycomb windows and explore traditional block-printing artisans in Sanganer.

3
Day 3: Scenic Drive to Udaipur (City of Lakes) Transit

Drive across the Aravalli hills to romantic Udaipur. Check into your lakeside hotel overlooking Lake Pichola, and attend an evening Dharohar folk dance show at Bagore Ki Haveli.

4
Day 4: City Palace & Sunset Boat Cruise Lake Cruise

Tour the sprawling Udaipur City Palace complex. In the late afternoon, embark on an exclusive boat cruise around Jag Mandir island with sunset reflections across the water.

5
Day 5: Royal Breakfast & Departure Departure

Relish a lavish breakfast on the terrace, pick up miniature Mewar paintings, and transfer to Maharana Pratap Airport (UDR) for your return flight.

The Little Details

Package Inclusions

  • 4 nights stay (2 nights Jaipur haveli + 2 nights Udaipur lakeside hotel)
  • Daily traditional breakfast + 1 authentic Rajasthani dinner
  • Lake Pichola sunset boat cruise tickets in Udaipur
  • Dedicated AC sedan with chauffeur for the entire circuit

Not Included

  • Interstate flight/train tickets into Jaipur and out of Udaipur
  • Monument camera and videography permits
  • Personal shopping expenses and tips
