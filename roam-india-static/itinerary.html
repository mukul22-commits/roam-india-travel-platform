The Himalayan Expedition Route (6 Days)

Manali · Solang Valley · Old Manali · Naggar Castle
₹18,900
Starting Package Fare
1
Day 1 — Arrival & Mountain Acclimatization Check-in & Welcome

Reach the mountain resort in Manali. Enjoy a warm welcome tea, settle into your valley-view room, and spend a relaxing afternoon adjusting to the elevation with a gentle walk along the bank of the Beas River.

Morning: Arrival & resort check-in
Afternoon: Rest & acclimatization
Evening: Riverside sunset walk & dinner
2
Day 2 — Local Heritage, Ancient Temples & Old Manali Culture & Cafes

Visit the historic 16th-century Hadimba Temple nestled in deep deodar cedar woods. Afterwards, cross over to Old Manali to sample artisanal wood-fired pizzas, herbal teas, and explore quaint local art shops.

Morning: Hadimba Temple & sanctuary walk
Afternoon: Old Manali cafe trail
Evening: Tibetan Monastery & handicraft market
3
Day 3 — Adventure & Snow at Solang Valley High Altitude Excursion

A full day excursion to Solang Valley. Ascend via the scenic gondola ropeway to 3,200 meters, take in panoramic glacier views, and participate in paragliding, zorbing, or winter snow tubing.

Morning: Scenic drive to Solang Valley
Afternoon: Cable car ride & snow sports
Evening: Return drive & hot chai break
4
Day 4 — Naggar Heritage Castle & Nicholas Roerich Gallery Medieval Architecture

Take a picturesque drive through apple orchards to Naggar, the ancient capital of Kullu. Explore the medieval wooden-and-stone castle overlooking the river valley and visit the Himalayan estate of Russian painter Nicholas Roerich.

Morning: Naggar Castle architecture
Afternoon: Roerich Art Gallery & local trout lunch
Evening: Valley viewpoint photography
5
Day 5 — Vashisht Thermal Baths & Mall Road Leisure Wellness & Shopping

Rejuvenate in the natural sulphur hot springs of Vashisht village. In the afternoon, explore the lively Mall Road, pick up authentic Kullu handloom shawls, pine nuts, and Himalayan preserves.

Morning: Vashisht temple & hot springs
Afternoon: Mall Road shopping & snacks
Evening: Farewell bonfire at the resort
6
Day 6 — Farewell Mountain Views & Return Journey Departure

Enjoy a slow breakfast overlooking the sunlit peaks, complete checkout formalities, and board your private transfer vehicle for the return journey.

Morning: Leisure breakfast & checkout
Afternoon: Transfer to transit hub
Evening: Tour conclusion with fond memories
